Pillar 1

Operational Excellence

Apr 2026 - S&P Global / Mining Journal
Anglo American seeks to lift Woodsmith production limits for polyhalite extraction. Regulatory approvals outstanding. £2.7bn cumulative spend confirmed.
Strategic impact

Operational Excellence starts with safety and social licence. A water contamination investigation at Quellaveco — Anglo American's flagship copper asset — challenges the foundational premise of the pillar. If the environmental challenge escalates to regulatory sanction, it disrupts mine planning and undermines the copper growth thesis simultaneously.

Committee question
Are water management and community consultation protocols at Quellaveco robust enough to withstand independent scrutiny — and is there an early warning system before the next investigation?
Pillar 2

Portfolio Simplification

↑ Rising Discuss Now

Botswana engages UAE and Oman sovereign wealth to acquire De Beers stake. Bid deadline pressure intensifies. Political dimension complicates sale structure.
May 2026 - Financial Times / Mining Weekly
Australian coal sale to UK firm confirmed at up to $3.9bn. Empres-rio raises competing offer for Brazil asset. Multiple parallel divestments under negotiation simultaneously.
Strategic impact

Portfolio Simplification is Anglo American's primary near-term value story. The risk is not executing individual divestments — it is managing concurrent transactions across three continents, with sovereign governments, competing bidders and regulatory approvals all running in parallel. A stumble in De Beers could dominate the external narrative and mask execution progress elsewhere.

Committee question
With De Beers, coal and Brazil assets all in divestment processes simultaneously, what is the board's view on sequencing risk — and how does external narrative around each transaction affect investor confidence in the overall plan?
